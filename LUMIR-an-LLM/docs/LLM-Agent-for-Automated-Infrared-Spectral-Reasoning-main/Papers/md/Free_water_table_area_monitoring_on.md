# Free water table area monitoring on wetlands using satellite and UAV orthophotomaps – Kampinos National Park case study

**Authors:** Maciej Góraj, Cezary Wróblewski, Wojciech Ciężkowski, Jacek Jóźwiak, Jarosław Chormański

**Journal:** Meteorology Hydrology and Water Management

**Year:** 2018

## Abstract

The surface water table level is a crucial factor for the existence of wetland habitats, and valuable from the point of view of environmental protection. In particular, surface water table in a hydrological year play an important role, affecting the seasonal changes in conditions of the development of species inhabiting a given patch of vegetation.
